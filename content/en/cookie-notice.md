# Cookie Notice

<p class="last-updated">Last Updated: {{ page.last_updated | date: "%B %-d, %Y" }}</p>

This notice covers **app.logicnodes.ai** only. The marketing website at logicnodes.ai may use different cookies; please refer to any notice presented there.

## 1. What Is a Cookie?

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your device when you visit. Cookies serve different purposes: some are strictly necessary to make a service work, others are used for analytics, and others for advertising or tracking across websites.

## 2. Cookies We Use

App.logicnodes.ai sets only the following cookies. Both are placed by Supabase Authentication, which we use to manage user sessions.

| Cookie name | Type | Purpose | Duration | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sb-[ref]-auth-token` | Strictly necessary | Maintains your authenticated session. Without this cookie, you cannot stay logged in. The cookie is HTTP-only, Secure, and SameSite; it contains only your session identifier — no other personal data. | Session (expires on logout or when the session token TTL elapses) | Contractual necessity — required to deliver the service you have contracted for |
| `sb-[ref]-auth-token-code-verifier` | Strictly necessary | Stores the PKCE code verifier used during the OAuth 2.0 authorisation flow. This is a one-time security token that protects against authorisation code interception attacks. | Session (deleted once the authorisation flow completes) | Contractual necessity |

No other cookies are set by the application.

## 3. Analytics — Cookieless

We collect basic page-view and performance data to understand how the platform is used and to identify issues. Critically, neither tool uses cookies or sets any tracking identifiers.

**Vercel Analytics** — first-party, cookieless page-view analytics. No cookies are set. No personal data is collected. Vercel Analytics is designed and documented as GDPR-compliant without requiring consent.

**Vercel Speed Insights** — cookieless collection of Core Web Vitals (page load performance metrics). No cookies are set. No personal data is collected.

Because no cookies or persistent identifiers are used, this data collection does not fall within the scope of the EU ePrivacy Directive's cookie consent requirements.

## 4. What We Do Not Use

We want to be explicit about what is absent from this platform:

- No advertising or behavioural tracking cookies
- No cross-site tracking or user profiling
- No Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager
- No Meta (Facebook) Pixel
- No LinkedIn Insight Tag
- No Hotjar, Mixpanel, Segment, or similar analytics platforms
- No third-party cookies of any kind

This is a deliberate product decision. As a B2B platform handling business-critical automations, we consider a minimal-cookie architecture to be both a privacy commitment and a security measure.

## 5. Your Choices

**Strictly necessary cookies** cannot be disabled without breaking core functionality. If you delete or block the `sb-[ref]-auth-token` cookie, you will be logged out and will not be able to use the platform. These cookies require no consent under the EU ePrivacy Directive because they are essential to a service you have explicitly requested.

**Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights** do not use cookies. There is nothing to opt out of at the cookie level. The data collected is anonymous by design and cannot be attributed to you individually.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings at any time. Common instructions:

- Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
- Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
- Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
- Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions
